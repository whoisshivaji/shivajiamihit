📖 How to Play Ludo

🎯 Objective: Be the first player to guide all 4 of your gotis (tokens) from your home base, around the track, and into the center home triangle!

🎲 Rolling a 6: You need to roll a 6 to bring a goti out of your home base. Rolling a 6 also awards an immediate extra roll!

⚠️ Three 6s Penalty: Rolling three consecutive 6s forfeits the third roll and immediately passes the turn to the next player.

⚔️ Capturing Opponents: Landing on an opponent’s goti knocks it back to their home base! You earn a bonus roll for every capture.

⭐ Safe Squares: Cells marked with a Star (and player starting cells) are Safe Zones. Gotis on safe squares cannot be captured.

🏠 Reaching Home: An exact roll is required to enter the center home triangle. Successfully bringing a goti home also awards an extra roll!
