How this editor works

  • Person segmentation: an AI model identifies the person mask.
  • Depth estimation: a second model estimates which areas are nearer or farther from the camera. Nearby chair/foreground pixels may be preserved when their estimated depth is close to the person.
  • Lens-inspired blur: the background is softened and blended around mask edges to reduce harsh cut-outs.

Privacy: image processing runs in your browser. The AI model files are downloaded from public model/CDN hosts the first time; your photo is not uploaded by this page. After the models are cached, offline use may be possible depending on browser cache and hosting.

Important limitation: a single 2D photo does not contain perfect real-world depth. AI may miss a chair, hair strands, transparent objects, or objects that overlap the subject. This is an automatic editor, so there are no manual selection tools.
